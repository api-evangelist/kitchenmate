# KitchenMate

> KitchenMate operates 24/7 unattended micro-markets for workplaces, condos, schools and
> hospitals, combining a checkout-free vision-sensor Smart Fridge with induction Smart
> Cookers that heat chef-prepared entrees in 7-10 minutes. The platform business has since
> rebranded as Micromart. KitchenMate publishes no public API, developer portal or SDKs.

## Company
- [KitchenMate](https://kitchenmate.com/): Product, menu and location marketing site.
- [About](https://kitchenmate.com/about): Company background.
- [Micromart](https://micromart.com): Successor autonomous-retail platform brand.

## Customer surfaces
- [Help Center](https://help.kitchenmate.com/en): Product guides, cooking and troubleshooting, menu and dietary information, loyalty and referrals.
- [Account login](https://my.kitchenmate.com/): Customer account portal.
- [Contact](https://kitchenmate.com/contacts): Sales and location inquiries.

## Notes for agents
- There is no public KitchenMate API, OpenAPI description, MCP server, webhook catalog or SDK as of 2026-07-19.
- `/.well-known/` paths and `/llms.txt` on my.kitchenmate.com return HTTP 200 but serve the AngularJS single-page-application shell, not real documents. Treat them as soft 404s.
- kitchenmate.com returns real 404s for `/pricing`, `/blog`, `/careers`, `/faq` and `/locations`.
